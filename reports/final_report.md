# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis Report

---

# 1. Introduction

The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn behavior and develop a predictive machine learning model to identify customers likely to leave a service.

Customer retention is critical for improving business profitability and long-term growth.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Analyze customer churn trends
- Identify factors affecting retention
- Perform exploratory data analysis
- Build churn prediction model
- Generate business insights and recommendations

---

# 3. Tools & Technologies Used

# 8. Key Insights

1. Month-to-month contract customers had higher churn rates.
2. Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to churn.
3. Higher monthly charges increased customer churn probability.
4. Long-term customers demonstrated higher retention.

---

# 9. Recommendations

- Encourage long-term contracts.
- Improve customer engagement programs.
- Reduce churn risk among new customers.
- Monitor high monthly charge customers closely.

---

# 10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates practical machine learning, customer analytics, and business intelligence skills using Python.

The analysis and predictive modeling can help organizations improve customer retention and reduce churn rates.
